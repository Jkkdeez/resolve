# Resolve demo runbook

## The one-line idea

**Resolve issues a Decision Receipt: an explainable, accountable answer people can act on—or an explicit handoff when it is unsafe to decide.**

## 90-second story

### 1. The problem — 10 seconds

“At SD Worx, the difficult part is not finding information. It is knowing which of several plausible answers is safe to use for this specific employee, country and date.”

Point to the live data pipeline. “Resolve turns scattered sources into contextual claims, detects conflicts, and makes ownership visible.”

### 2. Scenario A: trusted answer — 30 seconds

1. Select **A / Trust the answer** and click **Interrogate knowledge**.
2. Say: “A Belgian employee wants to work from Spain for eight days.”
3. Read the answer: “No manager approval is required.”
4. Point to the evidence ledger:
   - “The 2026 Belgian policy is used.”
   - “The old handbook is rejected as superseded.”
   - “The Netherlands policy is rejected because the jurisdiction is wrong.”
   - “The ownerless PDF is downranked.”
5. Point to **Resolve Receipt**: “This is our differentiator: not a confidence percentage, but a decision with receipts—what it says, why it is safe, and who owns it.”

### 3. Scenario B: safe refusal — 20 seconds

1. Select **B + C / Escalate & learn** and click **Interrogate knowledge**.
2. Say: “Now there are two current, official Belgian overtime policies: 150% and 200%.”
3. Read the refusal: “Resolve will not guess between authoritative conflicts.”
4. Point to the pipeline’s **1 open conflict** and Anna De Smet assignment.

### 4. Scenario C: organisational learning — 25 seconds

1. Click **Open Anna’s expert workspace** then **Confirm 200% premium decision**.
2. Say: “The expert resolves the customer-specific case once.”
3. Point to the updated state:
   - “The answer is now human-verified.”
   - “Open conflicts drops from 1 to 0.”
   - “Reusable expert decisions increases to 1.”
4. Close: “The next consultant gets the verified decision immediately, with Anna’s accountability retained.”

## Three judging messages to repeat

- **Simple:** one question, one Decision Receipt, one clear action or escalation.
- **Efficient:** Resolve does not send people through folders, Teams threads and inboxes; it brings the competing evidence together once.
- **Trustworthy and owned:** deterministic guardrails decide scope, jurisdiction, freshness and authority. A named expert owns unresolved decisions.

## Do not say

- “The AI knows the answer.” Say: “AI may assist extraction and retrieval; Resolve’s guardrails decide whether evidence is safe to use.”
- “High confidence.” Say what makes the answer safe: source, date, jurisdiction, owner and conflict status.
- “We integrated Teams.” Say: “We modelled Teams-style evidence; production connectors are intentionally the next deployment step.”
